Direct

Book directly with the apartment

The nightly rate is the same wherever you book. We do not undercut the platforms, and we are not allowed to. What differs is what gets added on top: the platforms charge the guest a service fee, and booking direct does not.

Booking direct also puts you in touch with SaasFeeHolidays.com, in the village, from the first message rather than through a platform inbox. That matters more than it sounds when you want a flexible check-out or an early key.

Worth knowing

Before you book

Who you are booking with

A privately owned apartment, looked after by SaasFeeHolidays.com, a management company based in the village on Untere Dorfstrasse. Not a chain, not a portfolio. One apartment, in one building, with one set of keys.

Payment and cancellation

Payment schedule and cancellation terms are set out by SaasFeeHolidays.com when they confirm your dates, before anything is due.

Tourist tax and registration

Swiss municipalities require guest registration and charge a small nightly tourist tax per person, collected locally. It is not included in the nightly rate and it is not a booking fee.

Still deciding?

The two things worth reading first are the room-by-room description and what Saas-Fee is actually like, particularly if you have not stayed in a car-free village before.
