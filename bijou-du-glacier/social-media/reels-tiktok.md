# Reels, TikToks and Stories — from the video you already have

You paid for a professional vertical walkthrough. It is currently used in exactly one place:
the hero collage on your own homepage, where only people who have already found you will ever
see it.

Cut up, it becomes a month of short-form video at zero marginal cost. The files are in
`video/`, ready to upload.

---

## ⚠️ Two things I fixed, and why it matters

**1. The original has the property manager's watermark.** `Saas-Fee house.mp4` opens with a
"VOLLA" wordmark over the glacier at ~2 seconds and closes with a "V" mark at ~31 seconds.
Posting that to your own accounts brands your apartment with someone else's logo.

Every file in `video/` is cut from the clean middle section (3s–29s of the master). No
watermark appears in any of them.

**2. I stripped the audio.** The original has a music track. It was almost certainly licensed
by the videographer for the manager's use, not yours — and posting music you don't hold
rights to gets your video muted, region-blocked, or struck.

All the clips are silent. **Add music from the platform's own library when you post** (the
Instagram and TikTok in-app audio catalogues are licensed for exactly this). It's one tap, and
it's also how you get picked up by the trending-audio algorithm, so you gain rather than lose.

## Practical notes

**Post the same file to all four platforms.** Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and
Facebook Reels all take 1080×1920. Don't make platform-specific versions; it's not worth the
time at this scale.

**Don't post with a TikTok watermark to Instagram.** If you download from TikTok it embeds
their logo and Instagram demotes it. Always upload the original file from `video/` to each
platform separately.

**Add captions/subtitles** in-app even though there's no speech — the auto-caption tool will
have nothing to transcribe, so instead use the text tool for the on-screen lines above. Most
people watch muted.

**Cadence:** one Reel or TikTok a week is plenty. Six posts here is six weeks, by which point
you should have new footage from the exterior shoot.

---

## The gap worth filling

Everything here is interior. You have no footage of: the walk from the apartment to the
lifts, the village at night, anyone actually in the apartment, or the building from outside.

A phone is completely adequate for all four. Thirty seconds of walking from your front door
to the piste, shot vertically on an iPhone, would be the single most useful new clip you
could add — because "four minutes to the slopes" is a claim, and a continuous unedited walk is
proof. Film it on the first morning of your next visit.
