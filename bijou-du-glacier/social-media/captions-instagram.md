## 6 — The design story
**Suggested post:** carousel — master-bedroom.jpg → bathroom.jpg → bedroom-3.jpg → kitchen.jpg

> Two things go wrong with alpine interiors.
>
> The first is too much wood — every surface carved, every wall pine, until the room feels like the inside of a cuckoo clock. The second is the overcorrection: a white box with a mountain view, which could be anywhere and feels like nowhere.
>
> We aimed at the space in between. Wide oak boards and a panelled headboard wall, but set against pale plaster and large-format stone. Brushed brass rather than wrought iron. Colour kept to the textiles — olive, rust, ochre — so it can change with the season.
>
> The bathrooms are where it shows most: big stone tiles, a band of dark slate, a floating oak vanity, a deep bathtub, and a backlit mirror. Modern, warm, and unmistakably in the mountains.
>

## Practical notes

- **Hashtags in the first comment vs. the caption:** either works on Instagram now. Keeping them in the caption behind a `.` spacer line, as above, is simplest and survives cross-posting to Facebook.
- **Facebook:** drop the "link in bio" phrasing and put the bare URL `https://bijouduglacier.com` on its own line instead — Facebook links are clickable, Instagram's are not.
- **Caption 5 is the highest-converting one and the riskiest.** It is fine to say this on your own channels. Do *not* put this language in your Airbnb or Booking.com listing text, in platform messages, or in anything you hand to a guest before checkout — that breaches OTA terms. See `strategy/marketing-plan.md`.
- Rotate captions 1–4 and 6 as evergreen; run 7 in September–October, and again in early January for late-season dates.
