## 6 — The design story

```
Two things usually go wrong with alpine interiors.

The first is too much wood — every surface carved, every wall pine, until the room feels like the inside of a cuckoo clock. The second is the overcorrection: a white box with a mountain view, which could be anywhere and feels like nowhere.

We aimed at the space in between. Wide oak boards and a full-height panelled headboard wall, set against pale plaster and large-format stone. Brushed brass rather than wrought iron. Colour kept to the textiles — olive, rust, ochre — so it can change with the season.

The bathrooms are where it shows most. Big pale stone tiles, a band of dark slate, a floating oak vanity, a backlit mirror. Two with deep baths, the third with a walk-in shower. Modern, warm, and unmistakably in the mountains.

## Posting notes

**Facebook version:** replace "Link in bio." with the bare URL on its own line — Facebook
links are clickable, Instagram's aren't. Everything else carries over unchanged.

**Hashtags:** keeping them in the caption behind a `.` spacer line works on both platforms and
survives cross-posting. Somewhere between 8 and 15 is the useful range; more than that reads
as spam and doesn't help.

**Which to run when:**

| Caption | When |
|---|---|
| 1, 6, 7, 8 | Evergreen — rotate any time |
| 2 | Evergreen, but strongest in winter |
| 3 | November–March |
| 4 | May–August |
| 5 | Once a month at most. It's the conversion post; running it often reads as pushy. |
| 9 | September–October, and again early January for late-season dates |

**Don't run 5 and 9 in the same week.** They're both asking for the sale.
